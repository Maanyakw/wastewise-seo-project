## 3. Niche and SEO Problem Identification

### Problem Statement

People frequently search online for practical information related to waste segregation, recycling, plastic waste, e-waste disposal, composting and household waste management.

However, useful information is distributed across government websites, educational resources, sustainability websites and industry sources.

WasteWise addresses this problem by organizing relevant information into a structured, user-friendly and search-engine-optimized information portal.

### SEO Problem

The existing information landscape contains useful resources, but users may need to visit multiple websites to find practical guidance for different waste-management topics.

WasteWise therefore focuses on:

- Search-intent-driven content
- Clear information architecture
- Topic clusters
- Internal linking
- Keyword-targeted pages
- Practical household guidance
- Authoritative references

### SEO Objective

The objective is to build a search-engine-friendly informational website targeting relevant queries related to waste management, recycling and sustainable household practices.

## 8. Competitor Gap Analysis

The research identified several opportunities for WasteWise.

| Existing Pattern | Identified Gap | WasteWise Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Individual topic-based articles | Information distributed across websites | Create a structured information hub |
| Composting-focused guides | Limited broader waste coverage | Connect composting with household waste management |
| Industry recycling content | Often technical or industry-oriented | Provide beginner-friendly household guidance |
| Government resources | Often policy/regulation focused | Present practical explanations with authoritative references |
| Academic resources | Can be more educational than action-oriented | Provide practical how-to content |
| E-waste information | Regulatory content can be difficult for beginners | Provide simplified disposal guidance |
