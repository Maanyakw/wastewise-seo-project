## 1. Milestone Overview

Milestone I covers the research, SEO strategy, project design, cloud deployment and initial implementation of WasteWise.

The project focuses on building an informational website that helps users understand waste segregation, recycling, composting, e-waste disposal and sustainable household waste-management practices.

The objectives of this milestone are to:

1. Identify a suitable niche, target audience and SEO problem.
2. Conduct keyword research and classify search intent.
3. Analyze competitors, search engine results pages (SERPs) and content gaps.
4. Develop a website architecture and prioritized SEO implementation roadmap.
5. Deploy WordPress on a cloud VPS and configure the domain, DNS and HTTPS.
6. Document implementation evidence and prepare a demonstration.

## 3. Niche and SEO Problem Identification

### 3.1 Problem Statement

People search online for practical information about waste segregation, recycling, plastic waste, e-waste disposal, composting and household waste management. Relevant information can be distributed across government websites, educational resources, sustainability publications and industry websites.

Users may need to consult multiple sources to understand different waste categories and practical steps they can take at home.

WasteWise addresses this information-discovery problem by organizing related subjects into a structured information portal with beginner-friendly explanations, practical guidance and references to authoritative resources.

### 3.2 SEO Problem

The project addresses the challenge of creating a discoverable, well-organized website in a broad informational niche. Important SEO challenges include:

- Identifying specific keywords and realistic content opportunities.
- Matching each page to a clear search intent.
- Organizing related topics into logical topic clusters.
- Avoiding duplicate or competing pages targeting the same query.
- Providing useful content rather than generic descriptions.
- Establishing clear internal links between related pages.
- Ensuring the website is accessible, crawlable and technically functional.

### 3.3 SEO Objectives

WasteWise will aim to:

- Target relevant informational queries related to waste management.
- Organize content around waste segregation, recycling, composting, e-waste and sustainable living.
- Develop clear page titles, headings, URLs and internal links.
- Use reliable references for factual and practical guidance.
- Build a technical foundation that supports search-engine crawling and indexing.
- Measure performance after the site has been connected to appropriate analytics and search tools.

These are project objectives; ranking improvements and organic traffic growth will need to be measured over time.

## 4. Target Audience and User Needs

### 4.1 Primary Audience

The intended audience includes Indian households, students, educators, environmentally conscious individuals and people who want to adopt more sustainable daily practices.

### 4.2 Audience Needs

Potential users may want to:

- Learn how to segregate household waste.
- Identify materials that can be recycled.
- Understand plastic recycling and responsible disposal.
- Learn how to dispose of electronic waste responsibly.

Content will prioritize simple explanations, step-by-step guidance, descriptive headings, examples and links to reliable sources.

### 5.2 Search Intent Classification

**Informational intent:** Users want explanations, instructions or practical knowledge. This is the primary intent targeted by WasteWise.

**Local or practical intent:** Users may look for nearby e-waste collection points, recycling facilities or waste-collection services. Location-specific content should be developed only when reliable local information is available.

**Commercial investigation:** Some users may compare composting products, recycling services or household waste-management solutions. This is a secondary opportunity rather than the website's primary focus.

**Navigational intent:** Users search for a specific organization, website or service. WasteWise will not prioritize this intent during the initial research phase.

### 5.3 Keyword Research Requirements

- Keyword.

- Source or tool used to obtain the metrics.

Search volume and difficulty should be attributed to the actual research tool and date. They should not be estimated or presented as measured data without evidence.

### 6.1 SERP Analysis Framework

The analysis considers:

- Ranking pages and their URLs.
- Content format and search intent.
- Topic coverage and page structure.
- Heading patterns and depth of explanation.
- Featured snippets and People Also Ask results, where observed.
- Internal and external linking patterns, where examined.
- Backlink information, where reliable data is available.
- Missing beginner-friendly explanations and opportunities for improved organization.

SERP features and rankings can change. Observations should be supported by dated screenshots or recorded source URLs when included as evidence.

### 6.2 Content Gap Opportunities

| Existing Content Pattern | Potential Opportunity | Planned WasteWise Approach |
|---|---|---|
| Information distributed across multiple sites | Related guidance may be difficult to find in one place | Organize content into a structured information hub |
| Content focused on a single waste category | Users may need related topics explained together | Connect segregation, recycling and composting content |
| Technical recycling-industry content | Beginners may need simpler explanations | Provide accessible household-oriented guidance |
| Policy-oriented government information | Readers may need practical summaries | Explain general practices and link to authoritative sources |
| Educational material | Readers may benefit from actionable steps | Use examples, checklists and step-by-step articles |
| Complex e-waste guidance | Users may need clearer disposal instructions | Develop responsible-disposal guides with reliable references |

These opportunities are working hypotheses derived from the research approach. Their effectiveness will need to be validated through content development and performance monitoring.

### 10.5 HTTPS and SSL

A Cloudflare Origin Certificate was created for the domain and installed on the Azure server. Apache's SSL module and HTTPS virtual host were configured to use the certificate and private key.

Apache configuration validation returned `Syntax OK`, and Apache was restarted successfully. The website was subsequently accessed through the custom domain using HTTPS.

Cloudflare's encryption mode was set to Full during configuration. The final deployment should be verified to ensure the domain is proxied through Cloudflare and the origin certificate is used as intended.

**Security note:** The Cloudflare Origin Certificate is intended for the connection between Cloudflare and the origin server. It should not be expected to be trusted by browsers when connecting directly to the server's public IP.

The private key, database password, WordPress administrator password and SSH private key must never be included in this repository or report.
