## 6. Search Intent Analysis

### Informational Intent

Users want explanations, definitions, instructions, or practical solutions.

Examples:
- waste segregation at home
- how to recycle plastic
- e waste disposal
- how to compost kitchen waste
- household waste management
- what is waste segregation

**Content approach:** Create educational pages, step-by-step guides, FAQs, and articles with clear examples.

### Local Intent

Users want services or disposal options in a particular location.

Examples:
- e waste disposal near me
- e waste collection near me
- plastic recycling near me
- waste collection services near me

**Content approach:** Consider location-specific guidance and verified collection resources in a future expansion. Do not publish unverified addresses or service details.

### Commercial Investigation Intent

Users compare products or services before making a decision.

Examples:
- best compost bin for home
- home composting bin
- waste segregation bins
- recycling services

**Content approach:** These queries may support future product comparisons or service guides, but they are secondary to the initial informational focus.

### Navigational Intent

Users want to reach a specific website, organization, or service. This is not the primary focus of WasteWise, although relevant organizations may be referenced in informational content when their details are verified.

## 10. Keyword Prioritization

Keywords will be evaluated using these criteria:

1. **Relevance:** How closely the query matches WasteWise's purpose.
2. **Audience usefulness:** Whether the content answers a real user question.
3. **Search intent:** Whether the planned page matches the user's objective.
4. **Search demand:** Estimated search volume in the target market.
5. **Ranking competition:** The difficulty of competing with existing results.
6. **Content feasibility:** Whether WasteWise can create accurate, useful content.
7. **Website fit:** Whether the keyword belongs on an existing or planned page.
8. **Internal linking potential:** Whether the topic connects naturally to other pages.

### 11.3 Findings

- **Broad terms:** “Waste management” and “solid waste management” show higher displayed volume ranges. “Waste management” is marked Hard.
- **Specific informational term:** “Waste segregation at home” has a displayed volume below 100 and is marked Easy. It is closely aligned with the website's purpose.
- **Topic-specific terms:** “Plastic waste management” and “e waste management” align with the planned Plastic Waste and E-Waste sections.
- **Composting:** “How to compost kitchen waste” aligns with the planned Composting Guide and is marked Medium.
- **Long-tail and local queries:** “How to compost kitchen waste at home” and “e waste disposal near me” may support focused guides. Local queries require accurate location-specific information.

These findings are preliminary. Search volume alone does not determine keyword value; relevance, intent, competition, content quality, and user needs must also be considered.
